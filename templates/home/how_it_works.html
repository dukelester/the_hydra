How TheHydra works

TheHydra is built around a single path: Search → Project → Evidence → Investigation → Report.

Evidence first

The source of truth is always the underlying evidence and source documents. The platform never invents missing facts, never auto-detects corruption, and never treats a citizen observation as verified by default.

Evidence coverage

Coverage measures how many recorded claims are supported by sources. It is not a corruption score, fraud score, or trust score.

  • Verified — a cited source supports the claim.
  • Partially verified — the source supports only part of the claim.
  • Conflicting information — sources disagree and further verification is required.
  • Unknown — evidence is unavailable or insufficient.

Official information and citizen observation

These are kept distinct. An official document and a citizen photo can both be recorded. Neither is automatically preferred as fact.

Reports

Reports are generated from structured investigation fields, not from an AI model. They remain private by default. Language stays neutral: potential discrepancy, evidence indicates, information unavailable, requires further verification.
