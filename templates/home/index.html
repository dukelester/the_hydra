The work

Follow. Find. Act.

Three moves on the public record — only as far as the files go.

How it works
  1. 01

    Follow the money

    Trace allocation, approval, procurement, contract, implementation, and completion — without filling gaps that sources do not support.

  2. 02

    Find the evidence

    Every claim should point to a source document, a page, and a verification status. If evidence is missing, H.Y.D.R.A. says so.

  3. 03

    Take action

    Submit a citizen observation, compare it with official information, and generate a structured accountability report.
